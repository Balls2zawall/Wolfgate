Legend: **SAME** = usable as-is/near-identical; **DIFFERENT** = exists but shaped differently,
needs an adapter; **MISSING** = no Wolfgate equivalent, must be authored; **COLLISION** = a

### 7e. Rating and sequencing for the wound-surgery slice

**Rating: heavy.** Not because any single step is hard, but because: (1) two component names collide outright and need a rename-or-extend decision before anything compiles; (2) the marker mechanism (string tags vs. typed components) must be translated for every condition, not copy-pasted; (3) every wound-specific condition/effect (`SurgeryHasWoundCondition`, `SurgeryClampBleedingEffect`, `SurgeryFractureGradeCondition`, `SurgeryMendFractureEffect`, `SurgeryTreatWoundEffect`, `SurgeryWoundedCondition`'s severity fields) is a thin shim over `WoundSystem`/`WoundBleedingSystem`/`WoundFractureSystem` APIs that don't exist before phase 1-2. Sequencing: **hard-blocked on phases 1 (bleeding/routing), 2 (fractures), and 3 (amputation-consequence wound)** — `SurgeryStopBleeding` needs phase 1; `SurgeryMendFracture` needs phase 2; `SurgeryHealAmputationConsequence` needs phase 3. `SurgeryTendWoundsBrute/Burn` (moderate, no incision) is the shallowest of the eight and could go first once phase 1 wound-severity numbers exist, ideally as a direct extension of Wolfgate's already-shipping `SurgeryTendWoundsBrute`/`Burn` rather than new prototypes.
